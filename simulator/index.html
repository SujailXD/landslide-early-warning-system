Landslide monitor

Simulator of the Arduino build: soil moisture on A0, tilt switch on D2, LEDs on D8 to D10, buzzer on D11.

The slope

Dry ground, pole upright
20%
70% or more counts as saturated
4.0°
The switch trips at 30°, when the ball rolls off

Start rain to run a storm: the soil soaks up water, the slope starts to creep once it is saturated, and the pole leans until the switch trips. Or drag the two sliders yourself.
